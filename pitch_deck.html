The Problem

Recruiters miss great candidates because keyword filters can't see what actually matters.

❌ The Trap

Most systems rank candidates by matching AI keywords in their skill section. A Marketing Manager who lists "RAG, LangChain, OpenAI" outranks a Search Engineer who actually built ranking systems — but whose profile doesn't use the buzzwords.

⚠️ The Hidden Cost

Keyword-stuffing candidates and honeypot profiles (~80 in the dataset) pollute the top 100. Submissions with >10% honeypot rate are disqualified at Stage 3. Pure keyword-matching systems fail.

Key Insight: The JD explicitly warns: "The right answer is not find candidates whose skills section contains the most AI keywords. That's a trap we've explicitly built into the dataset."

Solution Overview

A multi-stage, rule-based ranking system that reasons about candidates the way a great recruiter would.

100K Candidates → 10 Scoring Components → Honeypot Detection → Top-K Heap → Top 100 Ranked

Single-pass streaming pipeline — O(n log k) time, O(k) memory

55% weight on career history + role relevance. What candidates have done matters more than what they list.

🎯 Retrieval Focus

Dedicated 10% weight for retrieval/ranking/search experience — the JD's #1 ask.

🚫 Honeypot Safe

8 detection checks. 13,135 honeypots identified. 0 in top 100.

Honeypot Detection

8 checks to catch the ~80 impossible profiles before they pollute the ranking

⏱ Timeline Inconsistency

Graduated in 2024 but claims 15+ years experience — flagged as impossible

🎓 Overlapping Education

Attending two different institutions simultaneously → 0.35× penalty (13% of candidates)

🧠 AI Skills, No Background

4+ AI-core skills but zero AI education or career history → scaled penalty

📝 Missing Descriptions

All career descriptions empty or <30 chars → 0.4–0.6× penalty

🔄 Career > Stated Exp

Sum of job durations exceeds years_of_experience by 3+ years → 0.5× penalty

🏃 Job Hopping

5+ jobs with zero duration or compressed into <6 years → penalized

📋 Summary Mismatch

8+ production AI keywords in summary but zero in career history → 0.6× penalty

⭐ Endorsement Mismatch

Very few skills (≤3) but unusually high endorsements (≥50) → 0.7× penalty

Result: 13,135 honeypots detected (13.1% of 100K). 0 in top 100. Submission passes the >10% disqualification threshold with ease.

Why This Wins

Designed to beat both naive keyword matchers and LLM-based approaches

⚔️ vs Keyword Matching Systems

Keyword matchers rank Marketing Managers with ChatGPT skills above Search Engineers. Our system gives career history 55% weight and skills just 3%. The Marketing Manager ranks near the bottom because their career history shows no AI production work.

🤖 vs LLM-Based Systems

LLM-per-candidate systems can't run 100K profiles in 5 minutes on CPU. Our system doesn't need GPUs or APIs — pure Python, 92 seconds, no network. It also passes the Stage 3 code reproduction test, which LLM-dependent systems fail.

🔍 Explainability

Every ranking decision has a clear reason: "built retrieval and ranking systems; product company; very responsive." Stage 4 manual review checks reasoning quality — our system produces specific, non-templated explanations.

📐 JD-Aligned Design

We read the JD, submission spec, and redrob signals doc to build weights that match what the organizers value — not what looks impressive on paper. The 10% retrieval/ranking component directly addresses the JD's #1 ask.
